Travel Destinations

Four vertically arranged destination showcases with photograph, destination heading, and description.

Destination 01 • East Asia

1. Tokyo & Mount Fuji, Japan

Japan presents an extraordinary synthesis of futuristic artificial intelligence research centers and timeless cultural monuments. Visitors explore the snow-capped peak of Mount Fuji, sacred shrines surrounded by blooming cherry blossoms, and the hyper-connected high-speed Shinkansen railway network.

Highlights: Shinjuku Robotics, Mt. Fuji 5th Station Trekking, Pagoda Heritage
Destination 02 • Central Europe

2. The Alps & Geneva, Switzerland

Renowned for panoramic alpine vistas, pristine glacial lakes, and traditional wooden chalets nestled in verdant mountain valleys. Scholars participate in environmental sensor logging while hiking scenic trails overlooking the iconic Matterhorn and visiting the international physics corridors of CERN.

Highlights: Alpine Hiking Trails, CERN Particle Collider, Scenic Glacier Express
Destination 03 • North America

3. Banff National Park & Rockies, Canada

Banff encompasses some of the planet’s most pristine wilderness sanctuaries, characterized by emerald glacial waters, towering pine forests, and dramatic Rocky Mountain peaks. The destination offers world-class canoe expeditions, wildlife photography encounters, and undisturbed dark sky astronomical viewing.

Highlights: Emerald Glacial Canoe Routes, Rocky Mountain Wildlife, Banff Dark Sky Park
Destination 04 • Western Europe

4. Paris & the Seine Boulevard, France

Paris provides an unmatched cultural and architectural landscape defined by the iconic Eiffel Tower, timeless boulevards, and world-renowned museums. It serves as an epicenter for European technology summits, computational art exhibitions, and open-air riverside architectural photography.

Highlights: Architectural Drone Captures, Seine River Cruises, European AI Forums
